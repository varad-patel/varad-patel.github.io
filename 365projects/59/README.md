# Day 59: Comparative Advantage Trader

Set what two countries can produce, find who should make what and negotiate a price both gain from.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/59/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | If one country is better at making everything, can both still gain from trade, and at what prices? |
| One finding | With Ricardo's numbers Portugal needs fewer workers than England for both cloth and wine, yet both gain at any price strictly between 0.8333 and 1.125 wine per cloth, and at the market price of 0.9659 England is as well off as with 7.7% more of both goods and Portugal with 7.9% more. |
| Main limit | The model has one kind of worker who can change jobs at no cost, so everyone gains in it, while in a real country some people lose even when the country as a whole gains. |
| PPE concept | Opportunity cost, gains from trade |
| Skills | Ricardian model, linear constraints |
| Data | None. Everything is computed in the browser. |

## Files

* `index.html`: the page, including the write-up (the idea, how it works, what it cannot tell you, extensions).

* `app.js`: the interface, built on the shared kit in `../kit/`.
