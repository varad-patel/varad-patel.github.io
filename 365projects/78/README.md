# Day 78: Government Debt Dynamics

Set growth, interest and deficit and see debt-to-GDP paths.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/78/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | Where does a government's debt go as a share of GDP, given the interest rate it pays, how fast its economy grows and its primary deficit? |
| One finding | With US debt at 125.9% of GDP and a primary deficit of 3.24% in 2025, holding r and g at their 2025 values (4.02%, 5.00%) sends debt towards 347% of GDP, while their 2021 to 2025 averages (3.67%, 7.58%) send it towards 89%: the primary deficit that would hold debt steady is 1.17% of GDP in one case and 4.58% in the other. |
| Main limit | The projection holds interest, growth and the primary deficit fixed, while in reality they move with each other, with inflation and with the debt itself, so it shows where the arithmetic leads, not what will happen. |
| PPE concept | Fiscal sustainability |
