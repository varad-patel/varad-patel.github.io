# Day 72: Market Regime Detector

See the current market regime and how it has switched over time.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/72/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | Which regime is a market in now, how has it switched over time, and how far can the labels be trusted? |
| One finding | A three-regime hidden Markov model of the US 10-year Treasury yield since 1953 puts September 1979 to March 1988 in its turbulent regime and September 2026, the last month of data, in its normal one (99%), but refitted each year on the data available at the time it labels 14% of months differently from its hindsight labels. |
| Main limit | The regimes are inferred, not observed: the labels of recent months are the least settled, and a model with more regimes always fits better. |
| PPE concept | Market regimes |
| Skills | Hidden Markov models |
| Data | Federal Reserve Board, World Bank, Coin Metrics |
