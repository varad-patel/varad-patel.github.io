# Day 53: Social Mobility Markov Chain

Pick a country's transition matrix and see how a family's income evolves across generations.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/53/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | If children's incomes depend on their parents' as a published table says, how many generations does it take for a family's starting point to stop mattering? |
| One finding | In the United States table of Chetty and co-authors a child of the bottom fifth has a 7.5% chance of the top fifth; treated as a Markov chain, the table gives that family's great-grandchildren 18.4% against 21.5% for a family that started at the top, keeping 0.32 of a head start per generation. |
| Main limit | Beyond one generation everything rests on the assumption that only the parents' position matters, and studies of three and four generations find more persistence than that. |
| PPE concept | Equality of opportunity |
| Skills | Markov chains |
| Data | Chetty, Hendren, Kline and Saez (2014), Jäntti et al. (2006), IZA Discussion Paper 1938 |
