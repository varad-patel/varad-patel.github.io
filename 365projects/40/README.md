# Day 40: Supply Shock Simulator

Choose a shock such as a drought or strike and see price and quantity move and who gains or loses.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/40/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | When a drought, a strike, a tax or a trade barrier hits a market, how far do the price and quantity move, and who gains or loses? |
| One finding | With a demand elasticity of 0.3 and a supply elasticity of 0.5, a drought that takes a fifth of every harvest raises the price 52% and leaves sellers as a group 227 better off while buyers lose 486; with a demand elasticity of 1.5 the same drought costs sellers 54. |
| Main limit | Every result follows from the two elasticities typed in, which for a real market are known only roughly, and the tool looks at one market on its own. |
| PPE concept | Supply and demand |
| Skills | Equilibrium solving |
| Data | None. Everything is computed in the browser. |

## Files

* `index.html`: the page, including the write-up (the idea, how it works, what it cannot tell you, extensions).
* `logic.js`: the model, with no DOM code, so it can be tested on its own.
* `app.js`: the interface, built on the shared kit in `../kit/`.
* `logic.test.mjs`: tests for the model. Run them from the repository root with `node --test 365projects/40/logic.test.mjs`.

This file is generated by `scripts/build_data.py` from `meta.json` and `data/challenge.tsv`.
