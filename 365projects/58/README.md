# Day 58: Commodity Seasonality Decomposer

Pick a commodity such as onions or wheat and see trend, season and noise with a forecast for the next season.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/58/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | How much of a commodity's price is season, and does knowing the season help to forecast the year ahead? |
| One finding | India's retail onions run from 20% below trend in May to 35% above it in November, and on 840 overlapping held-out forecast months (81 different months) the decomposition forecast missed by 28.8% against 30.2% and 33.0% for two naive rules, a lead of 1.4 percentage points that is not tested for significance, while for US wheat the season is under 3% and the forecast (13.5%) did no better than repeating the latest price (13.4%). |
| Main limit | What counts as season is a choice of how much to smooth, and the forecast knows nothing but past prices, so a small edge over a naive rule on a few dozen held-out dates is not proof of skill. |
| PPE concept | Commodity markets |
| Skills | STL decomposition |
| Data | FAO Food Price Monitoring and Analysis Tool, World Bank Commodity Price Data (The Pink Sheet) |
