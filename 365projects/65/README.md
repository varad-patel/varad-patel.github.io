# Day 65: Risk Calculator

Enter a portfolio and see volatility, drawdown and Sharpe ratio.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/65/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | How risky is a given mix of Treasuries and foreign currency deposits, measured by volatility, worst fall and Sharpe ratio, and do the three agree? |
| One finding | Rebalanced monthly from 1999 to 2026, $4,000 in 10-year Treasuries with $2,000 each in euro, yen and Australian dollar deposits had a volatility of 6.27% and a Sharpe ratio of 0.08 and fell 21.9% from December 2020 to October 2022, while 20-year Treasuries alone had a Sharpe ratio of 0.18 but fell 42.3% from July 2020 to October 2023. |
| Main limit | The measures describe one past stretch at month-ends, and a Sharpe ratio over 332 months is uncertain by about 0.19. |
| PPE concept | Risk |
| Skills | Return statistics |
| Data | Federal Reserve Board H.15 and H.10, Bank for International Settlements |
