# Day 70: Fear and Greed Index

See an index built from volatility, momentum and market-breadth signals, with history and an explainer.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/70/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | Does a fear and greed index built from momentum, volatility and breadth tell you anything about where share prices go next? |
| One finding | With equal weights the index read 15 (extreme fear) in September 2023, but as a forecast of the S&P/TSX Composite's change over the next twelve months it did worse than the historical average in 553 out-of-sample forecasts from 1976 to 2022 (R² of −2.8%), and although the 88 months of extreme fear were followed by an average rise of 11.5% against 7.2% for all months, they fall in 26 spells, and counted once per spell, from its first month, the rise was 6.1%. |
| Main limit | The open data are monthly Canadian index closes that end in September 2023, so the gauge describes history, and its weights and ranking window are choices that a visitor can tune until the past looks good. |
| PPE concept | Behavioural finance |
| Skills | Composite indices |
| Data | Statistics Canada |
