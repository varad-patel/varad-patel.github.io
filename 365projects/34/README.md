# Day 34: Coffee Index and Big Mac Economics

Pick two currencies and see whether they look over- or undervalued against the price of a standard basket.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/34/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | Judged by the price of a Big Mac, does one currency look over- or undervalued against another, and how much of the gap is just a difference in income? |
| One finding | In July 2026 a Big Mac cost 236.25 rupees in India and 6.22 dollars in the United States, which makes the rupee look 60.5% undervalued at the market rate of 96.26, and 49.5% undervalued once the lower price that India's income predicts is allowed for. |
| Main limit | A burger is mostly rent and wages that cannot be traded, so a gap on the index says more about how rich a country is than about whether its currency is mispriced, and it is not a forecast. |
| PPE concept | Purchasing-power parity |
| Skills | PPP maths, regression |
| Data | The Economist Big Mac index |

## Files
