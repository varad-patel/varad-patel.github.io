# Day 75: Yield Curve Recession Model

See the yield curve and a recession probability with its historical accuracy.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/75/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | How well would a recession probability from the slope of the US yield curve have done if it had been fitted only on what was known at the time? |
| One finding | Forecasting 12 months ahead from the 10-year less 3-month spread with a probit refitted each month, the 525 forecasts made from January 1980 to September 2023 score an AUC of 0.83 (0.85 with hindsight); a 50% threshold catches only the 1981 to 1982 recession and calls one throughout January to September 2023, while 20% catches all five at the cost of flagging 61 of 473 other months. |
| Main limit | Five recessions in overlapping monthly forecasts are a thin test, and the 2020 recession counted as a hit was caused by a pandemic. |
| PPE concept | Macro forecasting |
| Skills | Logistic regression, ROC curves |
| Data | Federal Reserve Board H.15, NBER business cycle dates |
