# Day 37: Housing Affordability Map

Enter your income and see which cities you could afford on an interactive map, over time.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/37/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | On your income and deposit, in which districts of England and Wales is a home within your budget, and how has that changed since 1997? |
| One finding | On the median full-time salary of £39,059 with a £29,500 deposit and a loan of 4.5 times salary, the median home is within budget in 57 of the 318 districts of England and Wales in 2025 and in none in the south, against 285 in 1997 for someone at the same place in the pay ladder with the same share of a year's pay saved. |
| Main limit | The budget rule is a simplification of how lenders decide, and a district's median price is the middle of whatever sold there, not the price of any particular home. |
| PPE concept | Housing and inequality |
| Skills | Geospatial analysis |
| Data | Office for National Statistics |

## Files

* `index.html`: the page, including the write-up (the idea, how it works, what it cannot tell you, extensions).
* `logic.js`: the model, with no DOM code, so it can be tested on its own.
* `app.js`: the interface, built on the shared kit in `../kit/`.
* `logic.test.mjs`: tests for the model. Run them from the repository root with `node --test 365projects/37/logic.test.mjs`.

This file is generated by `scripts/build_data.py` from `meta.json` and `data/challenge.tsv`.
