# Day 35: Where Your Tax Rupee Goes

Enter the tax you pay and see it split across India's Union Budget, ministry by ministry, with ten years of change.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/35/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | If the tax you pay were spent like every other rupee in India's Union Budget, how much would each ministry get? |
| One finding | Of ₹1,00,000 of tax split like the 2026-27 budget, ₹26,256 goes on interest, more than Defence (₹14,674) and Road Transport and Highways (₹5,795) together; interest took 24.69% of spending in 2017-18 and takes 26.26% now, and borrowing pays for 31.7% of the 2026-27 budget. |
| Main limit | Taxes are not earmarked and borrowing pays for close to a third of spending, so the split is an accounting convention and not a trace of where any rupee went. |
| PPE concept | Public finance |
| Skills | Budget data parsing, treemaps |
| Data | Union Budget of India, Ministry of Finance |
