# Day 55: Unemployment and Okun's Law Explorer

Pick countries and see how output and unemployment move together over time.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/55/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | How closely does unemployment follow output, and is the link the same in every country and every period? |
| One finding | From 1961 to 2025 a year with one more point of growth went with a change in unemployment 0.44 points lower in the United States (95% interval -0.52 to -0.35) and 0.02 points lower in Japan; one slope forced on all seven G7 countries gives -0.13, which describes none of them. |
| Main limit | The slope records how output and unemployment have moved together in yearly data, not what a policy that added a point of growth would do to jobs. |
| PPE concept | Macroeconomics |
| Skills | Panel regression |
| Data | World Bank, OECD Economic Outlook No 119 |
