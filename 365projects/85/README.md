# Day 85: Kelly Criterion Lab

Enter your edge and odds and get the optimal stake with simulated ruin risk.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/85/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | How much of a bankroll should be staked on a favourable bet, and what do half, full and twice the Kelly stake do to growth and to the chance of ruin? |
| One finding | On a 10% edge at even money over 1,000 bets, Kelly's 10% stake ends with a median 150 times the start but a 47% chance of halving at some point; half Kelly ends at 43 times with an 11.5% chance; twice Kelly's median bettor ends with 0.87 of the start and 90% halve, while the average bankroll is 398 million times the start. |
| Main limit | Kelly needs the true chance of winning, which no bettor knows, and staking it on an overestimated edge is staking more than Kelly. |
| PPE concept | Risk and decision theory |
| Skills | Log utility, Monte Carlo |
| Data | None. Everything is computed in the browser. |
