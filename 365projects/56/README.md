# Day 56: Price Discrimination Lab

Split buyers into segments and compare one price, tiered prices and personalised prices on profit and consumer surplus.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/56/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Data Science").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | When a seller can charge different buyers different prices, who gains, who loses and what happens to total welfare? |
| One finding | With 100 students who would pay up to 10, 100 professionals who would pay up to 14 and a unit cost of 2, a price for each group sells the same 1,000 units as one price and lowers total welfare by 100; lower the students' top price to 6 and one price shuts them out, so group prices raise welfare from 5,400 to 6,000 and leave no buyer worse off. |
| Main limit | Every demand curve is a straight line that the seller knows exactly, and the result that group prices leave output unchanged does not survive curved demand. |
| PPE concept | Price discrimination, welfare |
| Skills | Optimisation, surplus calculation |
| Data | None. Everything is computed in the browser. |
