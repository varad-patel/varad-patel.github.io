# Day 82: Banking Contagion Network

Shock one bank and watch failures spread through a network.

Live at https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/82/ (part of [365 projects in 365 days](https://varad-patel.github.io/365projects/), section "Economics × Finance").

| | |
|---|---|
| Question | When one bank suffers a loss, how far do failures spread through a network of interbank debts, and how much depends on the network's shape? |
| One finding | With ten banks each holding a buffer of 6 and owing 100 to other banks, a loss of 40 at one bank fells six banks in a ring but only the bank hit in a complete network, while a loss of 70, above the system's combined buffer of 60, fells all ten in both and five in two weakly linked clusters. |
| Main limit | Failure costs nothing here beyond the losses passed on, the networks are regular and stylised, and unpaid debts are the only channel of contagion. |
| PPE concept | Systemic risk |
| Skills | Network simulation |
| Data | None. Everything is computed in the browser. |
